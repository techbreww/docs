## Before you ask for help

1. Confirm the server has premium if the feature needs it. Open **Configuration > Billing** to check.
2. Open **Configuration > Permissions** and fix anything BrewBot reports as missing.
3. Check that BrewBot can see and post in the channel you selected. Channel permission overrides can block BrewBot even when its role allows the action.
4. For stream audio, reload the OBS browser source after copying a fresh overlay URL.

Still stuck? Ask in the [BrewBot support server](https://discord.gg/WbMUYr7DCg). Include the feature name, what you expected, and what happened instead.
