BrewBot offers free Discord features and premium subscriptions. Open **Configuration > Billing** for a server's current plan, trial, payment details, and subscription controls.

## Standard plans

| Plan | Standard price |
| --- | --- |
| Free | \$0 |
| Premium monthly | \$30/month |
| Premium annual | \$300/year |

These prices match the [BrewBot pricing page](https://brewbot.gg/#pricing). Annual pricing saves \$60 compared with twelve monthly payments, about 17%.

Eligible new subscriptions have a five-day trial; the billing page shows the applicable trial and end date. Check that page and checkout for your actual price, including any existing, custom, or externally managed arrangement.

## Free features

- Reaction roles
- Welcome messages
- Mod logs
- Birthdays
- Basic Bot Settings and custom bot setup

AI backstory, chat, voice, and image generation require premium even when the bot's basic setup is free.

## Premium features

Premium includes channel and role managers, custom commands, reports, mod agreements, AutoMod, captcha verification, social alerts, leveling, polls, Brewlinks, Messages, and agent connections.

Premium [live tools](/features/live-tools) include monitors, transcripts, TTS and browser players, sound uploads and gift sounds, gift goals, timer gifts, Rate Me, game requests, music controls, overlays, and live-event tools.

<Note>
  Premium does not grant every team permission. Some tools require a team server, designated server admin access, Speed Dating access, or the separate Dare Grid permission. See [Teams](/getting-started/teams).
</Note>

## Manage a subscription

Use **Manage Subscription** to open the billing management flow. Team admins manage team-owned billing. A cancellation scheduled for the end of a billing period retains access until that period ends; check the displayed end date.

Personal servers may show **Stay on Free Plan** during the trial. Select the free plan explicitly if you want its limited features; an ended, canceled, or unpaid subscription should not be assumed to have become a free subscription automatically.

Externally managed subscriptions may direct you to support for changes. Contact [BrewBot support](https://discord.gg/WbMUYr7DCg) if the billing page cannot manage your arrangement.

## Slack billing

Each [Slack workspace](/integrations/slack) has its own trial and subscription state, managed from its Slack **Billing** page. Check that page for the workspace's available plans and actual price. A Discord subscription does not automatically activate Slack. Team token budgets can be shared when team billing is enabled.
