<PremiumNote />

The schedule assigns team members to live-show days. It requires a team server, premium, and Speed Dating access.

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of weekly schedule and publish controls */}

Open **Live Tools > Schedule**. Admins can add team members to weekdays and drag them to reorder the day's lineup. Navigate between the current week and the following week to plan ahead.

Keep changes as a draft while planning, then publish the week for non-admin team members to view. Past weeks are read-only. If you cannot edit the schedule, check your server admin permissions and whether you are viewing a past week.
