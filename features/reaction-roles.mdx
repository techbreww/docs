Reaction roles are available on the **free plan**. They let members pick their own roles by reacting to a message with an emoji. When a member adds a reaction, the bot assigns the corresponding role. When they remove the reaction, the bot removes the role.

## Set up reaction roles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Go to the [BrewBot Dashboard](https://brewbot.gg) and select your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reaction Roles">
    Open the **Reaction Roles** page from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new reaction role message">
    Choose a starting template or create a new reaction role message. Give it a title and optional description that tells members what roles are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add emoji-to-role mappings">
    For each role you want to offer, select an emoji and the role it should assign. Each message supports up to 20 reactions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the target channel">
    Choose the channel where the bot should post the reaction role message. The bot sends the message and adds the configured emoji reactions automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reaction modes

**Default** grants a role when a member reacts and removes it when they remove the reaction. **Reverse** removes a role on reaction and gives it back when the reaction is removed.

Use **Allow members to pick up roles from multiple reactions** to permit more than one selection. You can create channels and roles from the editor, then publish the message. Existing templates already in use are marked in the template list.

## Permissions

<Warning>
  The bot needs the **Manage Roles** permission to assign and remove roles. Its role in the server role hierarchy must be positioned **above** any roles it assigns. If the bot's role is lower, it cannot grant those roles.
</Warning>

<Info>
  The bot also needs access to the channel where you post the reaction role message. If that
  channel is private, locked, or inherits restrictive category overwrites, allow the bot role in
  that channel or category before you publish.

  For reaction roles, the safest channel-level permissions are **View Channel**, **Send Messages**,
  **Add Reactions**, and **Read Message History** for the bot role.
</Info>
