Birthdays is available on the **free plan**. Members save their birthday in Discord, and BrewBot announces it in the channel you select.

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of Birthdays settings */}

1. Open **Server Management > Birthdays**.
2. Turn on **Active**.
3. Select the **Announcement Channel** and save the settings.

Members use these commands:

```text
/remember-birthday date:03-15
/remember-birthday date:1995-03-15
/forget-birthday
```

The required `date` accepts `MM-DD` or `YYYY-MM-DD`. `/forget-birthday` removes the member's saved birthday for that server.

Birthday checks use UTC. During non-leap years, February 29 birthdays are announced on February 28. Make sure the bot can post in the announcement channel.
