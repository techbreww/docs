## Channel Manager

Create categories and supported text, voice, announcement, forum, or stage channels. Rename channels and edit role-specific permission overrides.

### Reorder channels and categories

- Drag a channel within its category to reorder it.
- Drag a channel onto another category, or onto a channel in another category, to move it there. The channel goes to the end of that category's text or voice channels.
- Drag a category onto another category to reorder categories. You can't place a category inside another category.

Discord sorts text channels and voice channels separately within each category. Voice and stage channels always appear after text channels. Dropping a text channel onto a voice channel in the same category does nothing.

When you move a channel into a category that has permission overrides, BrewBot asks how to handle permissions. Select **Keep Current** to keep the channel's permissions, or **Sync Permissions** to copy them from the category. Permission controls support **Allow**, **Inherit**, and **Deny**.

### Publish changes

Changes remain pending until you choose **Publish Changes**. Use **Discard** to abandon pending edits.

BrewBot saves the new channel order after your other pending changes. If any other change fails, BrewBot doesn't save the new order. After publishing, the list reloads from Discord. Check that your changes and channel order look right, and redo anything that didn't apply.

## Role Manager

Create a role from a template or enter a name and color. Edit permissions, whether it is displayed separately, and whether anyone can mention it. Drag roles to reorder them, or copy permissions from another role.

Use **Review** to inspect new, modified, deleted, and reordered roles before publishing. Roles above the bot, and other non-editable roles, cannot be changed by the bot.

## Permissions and deletion

The bot needs **Manage Channels** and **Manage Roles** for the corresponding operations. It can manage only roles below its own position.

<Warning>
  Deleting a channel permanently removes its messages and data. Deleting a category moves its channels into the uncategorized section. Deleting a role removes it and its permissions from members who held it. Review pending deletions before publishing.
</Warning>
