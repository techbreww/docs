Reports publishes a message and button in Discord so members can open your [Speed Tracker ticket form](/features/tickets). It is a premium feature.

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of Reports configuration and Discord preview */}

1. Open **Live Tools > Speed Tracker > Tickets** once to create the public form link, and enable the form.
2. Open **Server Management > Reports**.
3. Select the Discord channel for the panel.
4. Write the panel message, up to 2,000 characters. Plain text and line breaks are preserved.
5. Check the ticket-form destination and **Discord Preview**, turn on **Active**, and publish your changes.

The bot needs access to the selected channel and permission to post there. If the page says the form is disabled, enable ticketing before publishing. Manage incoming reports from the Tickets queue.
