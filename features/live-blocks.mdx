**Live Tools > Blocked From Live** maintains records of people blocked from live events. It requires premium, a team server, and Speed Dating access.

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of Blocked From Live records */}

Record the person's name, Discord or TikTok username, a reason category, and detailed notes. Categories include safety, harassment, disruptive behavior, underage concern, spam, no-show, and other.

Admins can edit active records or resolve them while retaining their history. These records are separate from the [TTS blocked-users list](/features/tts#audience-and-blocked-users); use the TTS controls when your goal is to stop someone's messages from being spoken.
