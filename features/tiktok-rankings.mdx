**Live Tools > TikTok Rankings** shows TikTok LIVE leaderboards, where your creator stands, and a full profile for any creator. It is powered by BrewAnalytics, BrewBot's analytics platform. This is a premium feature. You do not need to connect TikTok to use it.

## Your creator

The **Your creator** panel uses the TikTok accounts you track in [Chat Monitor](/features/live-monitor). If you track more than one, pick the creator from the menu; your choice is remembered in this browser. BrewAnalytics searches the rankings of the creator's own country, as shown on their TikTok profile.

- **League** shows the creator's league and rank in it, such as **B2 · #37**, the diamonds needed to reach the next place, how far they are above the last place listed, and how many creators in their league are live.
- **Daily** shows the creator's country rank from their TikTok profile, their place in today's top 99, and the diamonds needed to reach the next place.

The panel also shows today's diamonds, estimated earnings for the current period, and the last 30 days. Use **Show league** or **Show daily leaderboard** to open that board below.

When you open the page, the leaderboard starts on your creator's league, or on their country's daily board if they are not in a league's top 99.

<Note>
League lists only include each league's top 99 creators, so a creator below that shows **Not in the top 99 of any league** until they reach it. If a profile does not show a country, the United States is used.
</Note>

If no account is tracked yet, add one in Chat Monitor.

## Leaderboards

Choose a board, then filter it:

| Board | Shows | Filters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily** | Creators ranked by diamonds, with estimated earnings and live status | Region, and the current period or a finished day or week |
| **LIVE Teams** | Creators with fan teams, ranked by team points | Region |
| **Gaming** | Gaming creators ranked by diamonds | Region |
| **Shopping** | LIVE Shopping sellers ranked by TikTok's shopping score | Region |
| **Leagues** | Creators in one league, such as A1 or D5 | Region and league |
| **Global** | Top creators across all regions | Time window, from 10 minutes to 30 days |

Search a board by name or TikTok username. Select a creator to open their profile. Your creator's row is highlighted when they appear on the board.

Boards load when you open the page. Use **Refresh** for an update; requests within a minute share the latest snapshot. If a refresh fails, the page keeps the last successful results.

## Creator profiles

A creator profile shows:

- Estimated earnings for the current period, country rank, the last 30 days, the total since tracking began, and their best day.
- A chart of daily earnings for up to the last 30 days.
- **Recent streams**. Select a stream for its recap: earnings, diamonds, gifts, viewers, new followers, diamonds per minute, top gifters, and top gifts.
- **Top gifters** from TikTok's weekly gift gallery, with a menu to look at earlier weeks.

<Note>
Earnings are BrewAnalytics estimates in USD after TikTok's share, not TikTok payouts. BrewAnalytics starts tracking a creator the first time they are looked up, so a new or small creator may show little data at first. Their daily earnings fill in from then on. Stream recaps exist only for streams that were tracked while live.
</Note>
