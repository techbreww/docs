Gift Goals displays a selected gift, target, and current count on stream. It is a premium live tool available through the dashboard's Speed Dating access controls.

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of Gift Goals settings and overlay */}

1. Add your TikTok account to [TikTok Chat Monitor](/features/live-monitor).
2. Open **Live Tools > Gift Goals** and choose the **Monitored TikTok channel**.
3. Choose a gift from the gift picker.
4. Enter a **Goal title** and **Goal count**. The target accepts 1–99,999 gifts.
5. Copy the **OBS overlay link** into a browser source.

Settings save from the page. The overlay URL stays stable when you change the goal. Selecting a different gift resets its current count to zero.

Use **Current count** to correct progress manually, or **Reset count** to start again. These controls change the count shown on stream; they do not alter TikTok's gift history.

## Use with Speed Grid

The [Speed Tracker](/features/speed-tracker#gift-goal-on-the-grid) grid shows your goal's progress in its header. The monitored channel must be enabled for progress to appear there.

When viewers reach the goal, the grid asks whether to switch the panel. **Switch panel** returns panel participants to waiting and restarts the count at zero.

<Warning>
  Clearing the Speed Grid board also resets the current count to zero, including the count shown on stream.
</Warning>

After the goal restarts, the grid can prompt again when viewers reach it. Any of these restart the goal:

- Selecting **Reset count**
- Choosing a different gift
- Lowering **Current count**
- Changing **Goal count** or the monitored channel
- Clearing the Speed Grid board or switching the panel

See [Gift Sounds](/features/gift-sounds) to play audio for gifts and [Timer Gifts](/features/timer-gifts) to extend a countdown.
