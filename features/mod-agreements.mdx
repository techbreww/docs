Mod Agreements collects signed acknowledgments through invite links. It requires premium, a team server, and server admin access.

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of Mod Agreements invites and acknowledgments */}

1. Open **Moderation > Mod Agreements** and select **Create Invite**.
2. Enter a title and the agreement terms, then save the invite.
3. Copy its invite link and share it with prospective moderators.
4. Open the invite's agreements list to review names, Discord details, email, and the date each person agreed.

You can edit the title and terms, enable or disable an invite, or delete it from the invite list. This workflow records agreement acceptance; it does not automatically assign a Discord moderation role. Assign the appropriate role separately after reviewing the acknowledgment.
