Meeting notes is a **premium** feature. Run `/meet start` in a voice channel, or turn on [auto-join](#auto-join), and BrewBot joins, transcribes everyone who speaks, and posts a summary with key points, decisions, and action items to the channel you choose.

## Set up

1. Open **Server Management > Meeting Notes** and select the **Settings** tab.
2. Turn on **Meeting notes**.
3. Select a **Summary channel**. This is required. BrewBot must be able to send messages and attach files there.
4. Optionally limit who can start meetings, set a maximum length, choose the spoken language, and save.

## Commands

```text
/meet start
/meet stop
/meet status
/meet optout
/meet help
```

| Command | What it does | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/meet start` | Joins your current voice channel and starts taking notes | Allowed roles (anyone in the call if none are set) or Manage Server |
| `/meet stop` | Ends the meeting and posts the summary | The member who started it, allowed roles, or Manage Server |
| `/meet status` | Shows whether a meeting is running | Everyone |
| `/meet optout` | Stops transcribing you for the current meeting and deletes what was captured from you | Everyone |
| `/meet help` | Explains meeting notes | Everyone |

You must be in a voice channel to start a meeting. One meeting can run per server at a time, and stage channels aren't supported.

When a meeting starts, BrewBot posts a notice in the voice channel's chat with **End meeting** and **Don't transcribe me** buttons.

## Auto-join

Turn on **Auto-join** in the Settings tab to have BrewBot start meetings without anyone running `/meet start`.

1. Turn on **Auto-join** and add the **Voice channels to watch**. At least one channel is required; stage channels aren't supported.
2. Choose how many people must be in the channel before BrewBot joins (2 by default). Bots don't count.
3. Save the settings.

When a watched channel reaches that number of people, BrewBot waits 30 seconds, then joins and posts the same notice with **End meeting** and **Don't transcribe me** buttons. Anyone in the call can end an auto-joined meeting. Auto-joined meetings show **Auto-joined** in the Meetings tab.

If a meeting ends while people are still in the channel, for example because someone ended it or it reached the maximum length, BrewBot won't rejoin that channel until everyone has left. Only one meeting can run per server, so if two watched channels are busy at once, only the first gets notes.

## When a meeting ends

A meeting ends when someone runs `/meet stop` or presses **End meeting**, when it reaches the maximum length, or a minute after everyone leaves (if **End when everyone leaves** is on). BrewBot then posts the summary to the summary channel, with the full transcript attached as a `.txt` file if **Attach transcript to summary** is on.

The **Meetings** tab lists every meeting with its summary and a speaker-labelled transcript.

### If the summary can't be posted

If BrewBot can't post to the summary channel, the summary is still saved and the meeting shows a warning with the reason, such as a missing permission. BrewBot needs **View Channel**, **Send Messages**, **Embed Links**, and **Attach Files** (when the transcript is attached) in the summary channel.

Fix the permissions, or pick a different summary channel in Settings, then open the meeting and press **Retry posting summary**. BrewBot posts to the current summary channel. If the summary itself failed to generate, press **Retry summary** to regenerate and post it; this needs the transcript, so it isn't available after the transcript is deleted.

## Privacy and retention

- Audio is processed in memory and never stored.
- Transcripts are deleted automatically after **30 days**. Summaries are kept until you delete the meeting.
- From the Meetings tab you can delete just the transcript, or the whole meeting.
- Members can opt out at any time with `/meet optout` or the **Don't transcribe me** button.
- Other bots in the voice channel are never transcribed.
- Meeting starts, ends, opt-outs, deletions, and settings changes can be recorded in [Mod logs](/features/mod-logs#meeting-events).

<Note>
  If BrewBot restarts during a meeting, the meeting ends and BrewBot summarizes whatever was transcribed up to that point.
</Note>
