## Record and review

1. Open a TikTok monitor and choose **Record** or **Start Recording**.
2. Select **Stop** when the session is complete.
3. Open **Live Tools > Transcripts**, filter by TikTok account, and select **View**.
4. Review event totals and filter the feed by chats, gifts, follows, likes, or shares.

The detail page shows recording duration, total events, and time remaining before expiration. Use **Summarize** to generate an AI summary when there are recorded events, or **Regenerate** to replace that summary.

## Export and retention

Download **TXT**, **CSV**, or **JSON** from the list or detail page. Transcripts expire **24 hours after creation**, including time spent recording. Download a copy before expiration if you need a longer-lived record.

<Warning>
  Deleting a transcript removes it from the dashboard. Expired or deleted transcripts are no longer available there.
</Warning>
