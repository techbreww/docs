## Free and premium

**Free:** reaction roles, welcome messages, mod logs, birthdays, horoscopes, and basic Bot Settings, including custom bot setup.

**Premium:** AI features, meeting notes, moderation automation, custom commands, social alerts, leveling, polls, Brewlinks, reports, Messages, and [live tools](/features/live-tools), including TTS, gift and sticker sounds, and overlays.

See [Pricing and billing](/getting-started/billing) for plans and [Teams](/getting-started/teams) for access controls. [Slack workspaces](/integrations/slack) have their own installation and subscription.
