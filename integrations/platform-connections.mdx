Open **Configuration > Integrations** to connect platform accounts. Connection settings are separate from enabling premium alerts, music commands, and live tools. Team-owned shared connections may require a team admin even when you are a designated server admin.

| Connection | Used by | Setup notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok | Account-aware live tools and connected-account rankings | Authorize through the connection flow. Public LIVE chat and overlays do not require login. |
| Twitch | Twitch Monitor, TTS, and music chat commands | Search for a broadcaster and connect the matching Twitch account. Saved sources are shared across Twitch features. |
| Spotify | Spotify commands and track overlays | Connect a Spotify Premium account for playback commands. |
| Apple Music | Browser audio controller and music commands | Connect the account, then keep the audio controller open for playback. |
| OnlyFans | New-post alerts | Connect a creator through the OnlyFansAPI sign-in window. Connections are shared within the team and managed by team admins. |

## Reconnect an account

Check the connection status on this page when a feature asks you to reconnect. TikTok access refreshes automatically; reconnect when authorization expires or is revoked. Twitch may request another authorization when additional permissions are needed for Monitor events.

A connected account does not automatically turn on every feature. Continue to its setup page to select channels, enable the feature, and configure its audience.

See [Social alerts](/features/social-alerts), [Twitch Monitor](/features/twitch-monitor), [Spotify](/integrations/spotify), and [Apple Music](/integrations/apple-music).

For iMessage agent tools, use [Agent Connections](/integrations/agent-connections) under Engagement.
