<Update label="October 18, 2026" tags={["Live tools"]}>
  ## Per-category volume and `!volume`

  - New [Volume](/features/volume) page under **Live Tools** sets TikTok Chat TTS, announcements, gift sounds, sticker sounds, and Twitch TTS levels separately. Changes reach open overlays without reloading OBS.
  - Mods and trusted viewers can change levels from TikTok or Twitch chat with `!volume` (or `!vol`). It's off until you turn it on for a platform.
</Update>

<Update label="October 17, 2026" tags={["Server management", "Engagement"]}>
  ## Horoscope voice readings and meeting summary retries

  - New [Horoscopes](/features/horoscopes) feature. When a member joins the voice channel you choose, BrewBot offers a sign menu and reads that sign from your daily horoscope post aloud. It's available on the free plan.
  - When BrewBot can't join a voice channel, the error now tells you whether the channel is full or BrewBot is missing permissions.
  - [Meeting notes](/features/meetings) that failed to post can be retried from the dashboard with **Retry posting summary** or **Retry summary**.
</Update>

<Update label="October 16, 2026" tags={["Live tools", "Moderation"]}>
  ## Sticker Sounds, faster TikTok live alerts, and meeting mod logs

  - New [Sticker Sounds](/features/sticker-sounds) plays a clip when a viewer sends a TikTok sticker. Stickers are discovered automatically from your live, and you can use the same uploaded sounds as Gift Sounds.
  - [TikTok live alerts](/features/social-alerts) now post as soon as TikTok confirms the stream is live, even when chat is quiet.
  - TTS now converts stylized letters such as 𝓯𝓪𝓷𝓬𝔂 or ｆｕｌｌｗｉｄｔｈ text to plain text before reading, and skips decorative symbols.
  - [Mod logs](/features/mod-logs) can record meeting events, such as meetings starting and ending, opt-outs, and deletions.
</Update>

<Update label="October 15, 2026" tags={["Server management"]}>
  ## Meeting notes

  - New premium [`/meet`](/features/meetings) command transcribes a voice call and posts an AI summary to the channel you choose. Audio is never stored, and transcripts are deleted after 30 days.
  - Meeting notes can auto-join voice calls in the channels you choose.
  - [Pricing](/getting-started/billing) on the docs and dashboard now matches the landing page.
</Update>

<Update label="October 14, 2026" tags={["Live tools"]}>
  ## Battle mode for TikTok Chat Monitor

  - [Battle mode](/features/live-monitor#battle-mode) opens two TikTok lives side by side, with observed gifts, diamonds, likes, shares, and follows compared in the middle.
  - Recent gifts are now compact rows that show each gift's diamond value.
  - Chat monitors reconnect automatically after network drops or when your computer wakes from sleep.
</Update>

<Update label="October 8–11, 2026" tags={["Live tools", "Server management"]}>
  ## Speed Grid improvements

  - The [Speed Grid](/features/speed-tracker) shows sync status, keeps a shared **Activity** history, and adds **Vote off**, **Clear board** confirmation, and a **Move to slot** option for each profile.
  - [Gift goal](/features/gift-goals) progress appears in the grid header. Reaching the goal offers to switch the panel, and clearing the board resets the goal.
  - Redesigned profile cards, with adjustable font size and layout density in **Grid Appearance**.
  - MVP status can be changed from the edit profile dialog, and MVPs are managed from the new **MVPs** button.
  - Dragging stays smooth with large queues.
  - Channel and category reordering in the [Channel Manager](/features/channel-role-manager) now saves when you publish.
</Update>

<Update label="October 4–7, 2026" tags={["Dashboard", "Live tools"]}>
  ## Light mode and Rate Me screen time

  - The dashboard now has a light mode. Use the sun or moon button in the top bar to switch; your choice is saved in your browser.
  - [Rate Me](/features/rate-me) screen time can be set from 5 to 30 seconds.
  - Dashboard help links now point to this documentation site.
</Update>
